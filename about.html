01 / The people behind the promise

We believe
in each other.

Ananta is a collective of neighbors, educators, makers, and believers working toward a future that leaves no one behind.

Our beginning

It started with
an open door.

In 2008, a teacher in Jaipur opened her classroom after hours for children who had nowhere else to go. Ananta grew from that room, one relationship at a time.

Today, we work alongside 87 community leaders across 19 neighborhoods. Our job is to listen carefully, share resources generously, and make space for local wisdom to lead.
